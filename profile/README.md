# Eyleme

**Open source. Built with its community.** [eyleme.dev](https://eyleme.dev/) is the shared home for Eyleme, its documentation, examples and product family.

Eyleme is a standalone Apache-2.0 project for local, controlled task execution. Explore the [core project and current implementation status](https://github.com/eyleme-dev/eyleme), try the [examples](https://github.com/eyleme-dev/eyleme-examples), and contribute through the [public contributor guide](https://github.com/eyleme-dev/devguide/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). [Community discussions](https://github.com/eyleme-dev/eyleme/discussions), ideas and bug reports are welcome; change PRs follow the documented contribution and review process.

**Eyleme Derleme** and **Eyleme Onama** are optional private-source products following a paid subscription model. The family presentation is **Derleme → Onama → Eyleme**: prepare data, authorize work, execute. Basic access controls stay in the open-source core, and neither subscription is required for independent Eyleme use or community participation. Read the [public family overview](https://github.com/eyleme-dev/eyleme/blob/main/docs/en/product-family.md); commercial services and terms are in development, with pricing and availability unpublished.
